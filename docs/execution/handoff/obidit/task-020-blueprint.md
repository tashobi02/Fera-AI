## 5. Every paid call and revision rule

- Every live model provider call goes through runPaidCall once per dispatch, including repair and fallback. Do not wrap the whole adapter in a single reservation, and do not call LLMProvider.extract directly from the route. (obidit-spend-counters/src/server/spend/README.md:3,9-12; obidit-llm/src/server/ai/types.ts:21-26)
- ReservationRequest uses expectedRevision. A successful reservation increments ai_calls_used and revision once in reserve_ai_call; the route takes the returned reservation.revision as authoritative and signs that value. It must not also execute revision + 1 in the route. One repair or fallback is a separate reservation and therefore one separate bump. (obidit-spend-counters/src/server/spend/service.ts:17-19,123-132; obidit-spec002/db/schema_v2.sql:551-553; obidit-spend-counters/src/server/spend/README.md:10-12; _orchestration/agy-G5b.md:8)

Confirm whether cache access changes any display metric/revision; it must not be mislabeled a live call.
